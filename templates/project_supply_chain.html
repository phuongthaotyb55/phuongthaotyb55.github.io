Forecasts daily category-level demand and predicts late-delivery risk at order placement on ~180,000 supply chain order records. A residual Random Forest beats a naive rolling-average baseline by ~2% MAE. Shipping mode and the promised delivery window are the strongest drivers of late delivery (ROC-AUC 0.74).
